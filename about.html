01 / Outside of work

Balisong flipping

I enjoy balisong flipping. A balisong, also called a butterfly knife, has two handles that rotate around the blade. Flipping involves learning tricks and connecting those movements into sequences.

02 / Outside of work

Speedcubing

Speedcubing is solving a Rubik’s Cube as quickly as you can. My fastest solve was 13 seconds, though that was a while ago.

I’ve recently gotten back into it and am practicing to get close to that time again.
